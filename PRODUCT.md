# CreatorHub

CreatorHub lets clients discover published creator profiles, portfolio projects, and services, then discuss work through private inquiries. Public discovery is currently limited to Korea. Creators maintain their own profiles, projects, and services. Administrators use a separate protected dashboard to manage user accounts and roles, creator profiles, projects, services, categories, events, comment visibility, and saved website copy. Removed user/content records remain recoverable.

The source architecture follows Nestar-next: Next.js Pages Router, TypeScript, Apollo, MUI, shared layout wrappers, feature components in libs, and desktop/mobile SCSS. The backend contract in docs/schema.graphql controls every field and action. No payments, bookings, attachments, service thumbnails, or automated media moderation are introduced.
